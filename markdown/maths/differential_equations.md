# Differential Equations

for functions $y(x), p(x), q(x)$:
$$
y' + py + q = 0 \newline
y' + py = -q \newline
\mu y' + \mu py = - \mu q \newline
$$
$$
\mu' = \mu p \newline
\mu = e^{\int p\ dx} \newline
$$
$$
\mu y' + \mu'y = - \mu q  \newline
(\mu y)' = - \mu q  \newline
\int (\mu y)'\ dx = - \mu q  \newline
\mu y = \int - \mu q \ dx \newline
y = - \frac{1}{\mu} \int \mu q \ dx \newline
$$

$$
\mu = e^{\displaystyle \int p\ dx} \newline
y = - \frac{1}{\mu} \int \mu q \ dx
$$

y'' + py + q = 0 \newline
y'' + py = -q \newline

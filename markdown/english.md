

# Parts of Speech

## Noun
Name.

- **Common Noun**: generic names.
- **Proper Noun**: specific names.
- **Abstract Noun**: concept names.
- **Concrete Noun**: tangible object's names.
- **Collective Noun**: name of the group of X.

## Pronoun
Words in place of Noun, referred from the context.
- **Personal Pronoun**: refers to people in all of 1st/2nd/3rd person.
    - **Subjective Pronoun**: Subject of the sentence

        e.g. 'I', 'You', 'It', 'we', 'they'
    - **Objective Pronoun**: Object of the sentence

        e.g. 'me', 'you', 'him', 'her', 'it', 'us', 'them'

    - **Possesive Pronoun**: ownership

        e.g. 'mine', 'yours', 'his', 'hers', 'ours', 'theirs'
- **Reflexive Pronoun**:  Reflexive, refers back to the original subject. ('$\square$ self' words)

    e.g. 'myself', 'yourself', 'himself', 'herself', 'itself', 'ourselves', 'themselves'
- **Demonstrative Pronoun**: refers to specific things.
    
    e.g. 'this', 'that', 'these', 'those'

- **Interrogative Pronoun**: Questions.

    e.g. 'who', 'whom', 'what', 'which'.
